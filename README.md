# 🌿 Omie Heals

Your personal healing companion — body, mind, and soul.

A fully offline, single-file wellness website built for real transformation. No subscription. No internet required after loading. All data saved on your device.

## ✨ Features

- **Onboarding Quiz** — Enter your name, zodiac, feelings, goals, and health conditions to personalise your experience
- **Daily Checklist** — Morning, afternoon, and evening habits with streak tracking
- **Skin Tab** — Morning/night routines + weekly treatments. Nigerian ingredients. Fully checkable.
- **Body Tab** — Waist, glute, chest, and running workouts with YouTube video links + exercise tips
- **Food Tab** — PCOS-friendly Nigerian food checklist
- **PCOS & Fertility Tab** — Daily healing protocol, 3-phase plan, infections guide
- **Pelvic Floor Tab** — Kegel timer (squeeze/release auto-counter), pelvic exercises
- **Mind Tab** — Daily brain-growth checklist with free learning resources
- **Soul Tab** — Zodiac healing guidance, grief support, opening to love
- **Meals Tab** — 7-day Nigerian meal plan with daily cost (₦700–1,400/day)
- **Tracker Tab** — Visual calendar showing every completed day (April–December)
- **Journal Tab** — Private journaling with healing prompts
- **Daily Check-in** — Daily mood check that adapts your encouragement
- **5 Themes** — Dark, Light, Forest, Ocean, Sand
- **All data saved locally** — Your privacy is protected

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Free)

1. Create a free account at [github.com](https://github.com)
2. Click **New Repository**
3. Name it: `omie-heals` (or anything you like)
4. Make it **Public**
5. Click **Create Repository**
6. Click **uploading an existing file**
7. Drag and drop `index.html` from this folder
8. Click **Commit changes**
9. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**
10. Under **Source**, select `main` branch → `/root`
11. Click **Save**
12. Your site will be live at: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/omie-heals`

That's it! Free forever. Share the link with anyone you want.

## 📱 Using on Your Phone

Once hosted, open the link on your phone.
- On iPhone: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- On Android: Chrome → Menu → **Add to Home Screen**

It will work like an app icon on your phone!

## 🔒 Data & Privacy

All your journal entries, streaks, and check-ins are saved to your device's local storage only. Nothing is sent to any server. Your healing is private.
