Infections & Symptoms

Common Infections & Natural Support

  • BV / Vaginal odour: Eat plain yogurt (probiotics), avoid scented soaps near vagina, wear cotton underwear. See a doctor for recurring cases.
  • UTI (burning when urinating): Drink lots of water, unsweetened cranberry juice, see a doctor promptly.
  • Yeast infection: Reduce sugar drastically, apply plain yogurt externally temporarily, see a doctor. PCOS raises yeast risk.
  • Irregular period: The PCOS daily protocol above (cinnamon, exercise, reduce sugar) is your best natural tool.
  • IMPORTANT: Always see a doctor for infections — natural remedies support but do not replace medical care.

What are Kegels?

Understanding Pelvic Floor Exercises

Kegel exercises contract and release your pelvic floor muscles — the same muscles you use to stop urine mid-flow. Doing them daily tightens the vagina, strengthens bladder control, reduces prolapse risk, and improves sensation. Results appear within 6–12 weeks of daily practice. They can be done anywhere, no one can tell.
